The Structure of Relation Decoding Linear Operators in Large Language Models
Miranda Anna Christ, Adrian Csiszarik, Gergely Becso, Daniel Varga
NeurIPS (Spotlight), 2025
Paper / Code

Hernandez et al. showed that a single linear map can decode a relation like capital city of country or food of country in LLMs. We study these maps collectively and find that they encode coarse-grained properties like country rather than individual relations, making them compressible (~800×) and sometimes generalizable with tensor networks.

Towards Human-AI Complementarity for AI Safety
Sander Lanbo Shi*, Miranda Anna Christ*, Eric Modesitt, ..., Konstantinos Voudouris, David Demitri Africa, Joshua Jacob, Rishub Jain (*First authors)
Under review, 2026
Paper / Code

We release the largest known benchmark of safety-relevant tasks for human–AI complementarity (3,957 items) and collect 35,613 human judgements. Humans correct AI on 12.7% of items, a large complementarity opportunity that the white- and black-box routers and assistance methods we test do not realize. Complementarity is feasible, but needs assistance that keeps human corrections independent and routers that know when the model is wrong.

Toward Human-AI Complementarity Across Diverse Tasks
Yuzheng Xu, Annya Dahmani, ..., Miranda Anna Christ, ..., Rishub Jain
NSF AI-SDM Workshop on Human-AI Complementarity (Talk), 2026
Paper / Code

In scalable oversight, human and AI judges sometimes disagree. Human–AI complementarity tries to leverage this by routing each question to whoever is more likely to be right. Testing a confidence-based router across four domains, we find this hard to achieve, as model confidence is poorly calibrated and humans over-rely on AI assistance.

Small Features Matter: Robust Representation for World Models
Zarif Ikram, Miranda Anna Christ, Ling Pan, Dianbo Liu
ICLR Robot Learning Workshop, 2025
Paper

World models trained by pixel reconstruction can miss small but task-critical features, like the ball in the Atari game Breakout. We add a joint-embedding forward prediction loss to DreamerV3 so the encoder must capture what predicts the future. We outperform or match DreamerV3 on 18/26 Atari100k games and stay robust under pixel noise and distracting backgrounds.
